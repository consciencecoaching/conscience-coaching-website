Wellbeing walks: frequently asked questions

What are wellbeing walks?

Our wellbeing walks offer an opportunity to step away from the busyness of everyday life, spend time outdoors, and reconnect with yourself, nature, and other people.

They provide a welcoming, supportive, and non-judgemental space to walk, talk, reflect and enjoy the natural environment. Our walks are about more than simply getting from A to B; they're about making time for yourself, building connections and supporting your wellbeing.

Who can take part in a wellbeing walk?

Our wellbeing walks are for any adults who would like to take some time for themselves, enjoy the outdoors, or connect with others.

Whether you're feeling stressed, overwhelmed or isolated, or simply looking for a positive experience in nature, you're welcome. You don't need to have a particular concern or reason for joining us.

Do I need to be fit or an experienced walker?

Not at all! Our walks are about wellbeing, connection and enjoying the outdoors rather than speed or distance.

Each walk is different, so please check the individual walk details before booking. These include information about the location, time, terrain, distance, and difficulty level to help you decide whether the walk is right for you.

If you're unsure about your suitability for a particular walk, please get in touch before booking.

What happens during a wellbeing walk?

Each walk offers an opportunity to enjoy nature, take a break from everyday pressures, and connect with yourself and others.

There may be opportunities for conversation, reflection and simply noticing the world around you. Every walk is different, and there is no pressure to participate in any activity that doesn't feel right for you.

You can chat, listen, reflect, or simply enjoy the surroundings at your own pace.

Do I need to talk about my feelings or personal circumstances?

Absolutely not. You're welcome to share as much or as little as you feel comfortable with.

Some people enjoy chatting with others, while others prefer to walk quietly and take in their surroundings. There's no expectation to share personal experiences, and you can take part in whatever way feels right for you.

Can I come on my own?

Of course! You're very welcome to join us on your own.

Meeting new people or joining a group for the first time can sometimes feel daunting, but we aim to create a friendly, inclusive and non-judgemental environment where everyone feels welcome.

You don't need to know anyone before you arrive. Our walks are also an opportunity to meet new people and develop connections in a relaxed outdoor setting.

What are the benefits of a wellbeing walk?

Spending time outdoors and connecting with others can provide an opportunity to pause, reflect, clear your mind and gain a fresh perspective.

People may experience a greater sense of calm, improved mood, reduced feelings of stress, increased appreciation of nature or a stronger sense of connection and belonging.

Everyone's experience is different, and there is no right or wrong way to benefit from a walk. Sometimes, simply taking time out of your usual routine can make a difference.

Are wellbeing walks a form of therapy or counselling?

Our wellbeing walks provide non-clinical wellbeing support and opportunities for reflection, connection, and time outdoors. They are not a substitute for counselling, psychotherapy, or professional mental health treatment.

If you need clinical or specialist support, we encourage you to seek help from an appropriately qualified professional.

How much does it cost to attend a wellbeing walk?

Our wellbeing walks are offered on a give-as-you-feel basis because accessibility matters to us.

We want people to have the opportunity to benefit from time outdoors and connection with others, regardless of their financial circumstances. You're welcome to contribute an amount that feels right for you, with no pressure or judgement. The suggested contribution is £10.

Can I get a lift to the walk?

We recognise that transport can sometimes be a barrier to taking part. To help make our walks more accessible, we encourage and facilitate car sharing.

Car-share arrangements are co-ordinated through a dedicated WhatsApp group that is set up before the walk. Sharing a journey is a great way to meet fellow walkers, reduce travel costs, and help reduce our environmental impact.

If you would like a lift or are happy to offer one, the group provides an opportunity to connect with other participants and explore arrangements.

If you don't use WhatsApp, please contact us directly and we'll do our best to help.

Although we encourage car sharing, we cannot guarantee that a lift will always be available.

Do I need to join the WhatsApp group?

No, joining the WhatsApp group is entirely optional.

A dedicated WhatsApp group is set up approximately two weeks before each walk to help participants connect, arrange car shares, share photos, and receive important updates.

If you prefer not to join the group, you'll receive updates by text message instead, so you won't miss essential information about the walk.

How do you check that a walk is safe to go ahead?

A safety check is carried out approximately one week before each walk to review conditions and any relevant factors that could affect the group's safety.

We aim to make the walks as safe and enjoyable as possible. If circumstances change, we will consider adjustments or alternatives wherever possible.

What happens if the weather is bad or conditions change?

Our preference is always to explore ways to adapt a walk safely rather than cancel it.

If conditions change, we will consider appropriate adjustments or alternative arrangements wherever possible. Any changes will be communicated through the dedicated WhatsApp group, with separate text updates for anyone who hasn't joined.

Cancellation is a last resort when a safe alternative cannot be found.

Will photos be taken during the wellbeing walks?

We love capturing and celebrating the moments, connections and beautiful surroundings experienced on our wellbeing walks. Where consent has been given, photos may be taken and shared in the dedicated WhatsApp group by all on the walk.

We ask for your photo consent as part of the booking process, so you can let us know your preferences before attending. Giving consent is entirely your choice, and there is no pressure to be photographed.

You can withdraw your consent at any time by emailing Angela at

angela@consciencecoaching.co.uk.

We want everyone to feel comfortable, respected and able to enjoy the walk in their own way.

Can I bring my dog?

Yes, dogs are welcome on our wellbeing walks, provided they are suitable for the planned walk, can be managed safely around other participants and the other participants agree.

The adult responsible for the dog must ensure its behaviour is appropriate and that it remains under control throughout the walk. Please consider the location, time, terrain, distance, and difficulty level when deciding whether the walk is suitable for your dog.

Can I bring my children?

Yes, children are welcome on our wellbeing walks, provided they are suitable for the planned walk, can be managed safely around other participants and the other participants agree.

The adult registered to attend remains fully responsible for the children in their care throughout the walk, including their safety, supervision and behaviour. Please consider the location, time, terrain, distance, and difficulty level when deciding whether the walk is suitable for your child.

Are the walks accessible for people with different needs or mobility levels?

Accessibility and inclusion matter to us. We want to make it as easy as possible for people to take part, recognising that everyone's needs and circumstances are different.

As routes and terrain vary, accessibility will depend on the individual walk. Please read the walk details before booking and get in touch if you have any accessibility requirements, mobility considerations or concerns about the route or transport.

We'll be happy to discuss your needs and explore what may be possible.

What should I wear or bring?

We recommend wearing comfortable clothing suitable for the weather and footwear appropriate for the planned route and terrain.

Depending on the forecast and the length of the walk, you may also wish to bring a waterproof jacket, water, sun protection and any other essentials you need to feel comfortable.

Packed lunch, snacks and plenty of water is also recommended.

Please check the individual walk details for any additional requirements.

Will what I share during a walk remain confidential?

We aim to create a respectful and supportive environment where people feel comfortable being themselves.

We ask everyone taking part to respect other participants' privacy and personal experiences.

However, we cannot guarantee that other participants will keep everything shared confidential, so please only share what you feel comfortable discussing in a group setting.

If evidence of illegal activity or the potential for harm to yourself or others is disclosed, we may have to inform the appropriate authorities. We will firstly seek your consent and permission wherever possible and appropriate.

What if I feel nervous about joining my first walk?

That's completely understandable! Trying something new or meeting new people can feel daunting, especially if you've been feeling anxious, overwhelmed or isolated.

You don't need to arrive feeling confident, and you don't need to have everything figured out.

Our walks are intended to be welcoming and non-judgemental, with no pressure to talk or share personal experiences.

Simply turning up and giving yourself the opportunity to take part is a positive first step.

Come as you are.

How do I book a place on a wellbeing walk?

Please visit the events page on our website and explore the available wellbeing walks. Each walk has its own details, including the time, terrain, distance and difficulty level, to help you decide whether it's suitable for you.

The walks are hosted on the Plinth platform - follow the booking instructions provided for your chosen walk. If you have any questions before booking, please get in touch.

What if I have more questions before attending?

We'd love to hear from you!

If you're unsure whether a wellbeing walk is right for you, have questions about accessibility or car sharing, or would simply like to find out more before joining, please contact Angela at angela@consciencecoaching.co.uk.

We're happy to have a conversation and help you decide whether a wellbeing walk is a good fit for you.

Ready to take the first step?

You don't need to be an experienced walker, feel completely confident, or know exactly what you need.

Sometimes, all it takes is a little time outdoors, a welcoming space and the opportunity to connect.

Come as you are, take a breath and give yourself permission to join us.

Explore our wellbeing walks on our events page at consciencecoaching.co.uk and find an opportunity to make a little space for your wellbeing.

Wellbeing retreats: frequently asked questions

What is a wellbeing retreat?

Our wellbeing retreats offer an opportunity to step away from the busyness of everyday life, take a break from your usual routine so that you can focus on your wellbeing and what matters to you.

Taking place over two days, and including an overnight stay, they provide time and space to enjoy nature, connect with others, reflect and gain a fresh perspective. Our retreats are about more than simply getting away; they're about giving yourself permission to pause, reconnect and make time for yourself.

Who can take part in a wellbeing retreat?

Our wellbeing retreats are for any adults who would like to take some time for themselves, enjoy new experiences outdoors, or connect with others.

Whether you're feeling stressed, overwhelmed or isolated, going through a period of change, or simply looking for a positive experience away from everyday life, you're welcome. You don't need to have a particular concern or reason for joining us.

What happens at a retreat?

Every retreat is different, depending on the location and planned activities.

There will be opportunities to step away from everyday pressures and enjoy time for yourself.

Depending on the retreat, this may include spending time outdoors, connecting with nature, walking, conversation, reflection, and shared experiences with other participants.

There is no pressure to participate in any activity that doesn't feel right for you. You can chat, listen, reflect, or simply enjoy the surroundings at your own pace.

Safety is our top priority, and all retreats are designed with this as our firm foundation – they are safe socially, psychologically, emotionally and physically. This does not mean they are comfortable – getting out of comfort zones allows us to grow, and we provide that opportunity at the retreats.

Please check the individual retreat details for the itinerary, activities, and timings.

Do I need to be fit or an experienced walker?

Not necessarily! Our retreats are about wellbeing, connection, and enjoying new experiences rather than speed or distance.

However, activities and walking routes may vary between retreats. Please check the individual retreat details for information about the terrain, walking distances, and difficulty level to help you decide whether the experience is right for you.

If you're unsure about your suitability for a particular retreat, please get in touch before booking.

Can I come on my own?

Of course! You're very welcome to join us on your own.

Meeting new people or joining a group for the first time can sometimes feel daunting, but we aim to create a friendly, inclusive and non-judgemental environment where everyone feels welcome.

You don't need to know anyone before you arrive. Our retreats are also an opportunity to meet new people and develop connections in a relaxed outdoor setting.

Do I need to talk about my feelings or personal circumstances?

Absolutely not. You're welcome to share as much or as little as you feel comfortable with.

We want you to feel comfortable, respected, and able to enjoy the retreat in your own way.

What are the benefits of a wellbeing retreat?

Taking time away from everyday pressures, spending time outdoors and connecting with others can provide an opportunity to pause, clear your mind, and gain a fresh perspective.

People may experience a greater sense of calm, improved mood, reduced feelings of stress, increased appreciation of nature, or a stronger sense of connection and belonging.

A retreat may also give you space to consider what matters to you and what you would like to take forward into everyday life.

Everyone's experience is different, and there is no right or wrong way to benefit from a retreat. Sometimes, simply giving yourself permission to take time out can make a difference.

Are wellbeing retreats a form of therapy or counselling?

Our wellbeing retreats provide non-clinical wellbeing support and opportunities for reflection, connection, and time outdoors. They are not a substitute for counselling, psychotherapy, or professional mental health treatment.

If you need clinical or specialist support, we encourage you to seek help from an appropriately qualified professional.

Whilst not therapy, they can be therapeutic and can be complementary to any clinical or specialist support you may be receiving.

Where will we stay, and what is the accommodation like?

The location and accommodation arrangements will be provided in the individual retreat details.

As each retreat may be different, please check the event information for details about the accommodation, sleeping arrangements, facilities and anything else you may need to know before booking.

If you have any questions or specific requirements, please get in touch before confirming your place.

How much does a wellbeing retreat cost, and what is included?

The price and inclusions will be outlined in the individual retreat listing.

Whilst our mission is for people to have the opportunity to benefit from time outdoors and connection with others, regardless of their financial circumstances, we are not able to offer the retreats on a give-as-you-feel basis like we do our wellbeing walks. The price is set to cover the costs of the retreat, whilst also being mindful of our commitment to accessibility.

Please check the booking information carefully to see what is included in the cost, such as accommodation, activities, meals and refreshments, and whether any additional expenses need to be considered.

If you have any questions about the cost or accessibility of a retreat, please get in touch.

Please check the individual retreat details for any additional recommendations, including anything needed for the overnight stay or planned activities.
