Nature-based coaching

Support shaped around what matters to you

Outdoor, solution-focused coaching for individuals, groups and teams facing change, pressure or questions about what comes next.

How the coaching works

Conscience coaching takes place outdoors in a safe and supportive environment. The work starts with a free introductory chemistry session, giving you and Angela a chance to decide whether the coaching relationship feels right.

This is followed by up to six one-hour coaching sessions at an agreed outdoor location. Sessions may be one-to-one or designed for a group or team.

The approach is solution-focused. You will identify strengths, gain clarity and work towards positive actions and achievable goals, rather than staying fixed on the problem.

Non-clinical support

Coaching, not treatment

Conscience provides coaching and wellbeing support. It does not diagnose conditions or provide clinical mental-health treatment.

What sessions can explore

Work, wellbeing and change

Stress and resilience

Understand what is creating pressure and find practical ways to respond.

Confidence and self-awareness

Recognise your strengths, understand your patterns and make decisions with greater confidence.

Work and balance

Explore work-life balance, remaining in work or returning after time away.

Change and direction

Think through change at work or in everyday life, including a possible change of career or direction.

Between sessions

Follow-up reflections, resources and email support are available between sessions. These can help you hold on to useful thoughts and continue the work in everyday life.

What people report

Participants often report better mental wellbeing, more self-awareness and confidence, less stress, greater resilience and clarity, and a stronger connection with nature.

Coaching frequently asked questions

What is coaching with Conscience Coaching and Consulting?

Coaching with Conscience offers an opportunity to step back from everyday pressures, reflect on your current situation and explore what you would like to be different.

Our approach is person-centred and solution-focused. Rather than telling you what to do, coaching supports you to recognise your strengths, explore your options, and identify steps that feel right for you.

You don't need to have all the answers before you begin. Sometimes, having time and space to think is the first step towards change.

Who is coaching for?

Coaching is for anyone who would value time to reflect, gain clarity, or explore possibilities for the future.

You might be experiencing change, feeling stuck, facing a difficult decision, looking to build confidence, or wanting to reconnect with what matters to you.

You don't need to be experiencing difficulties or have a particular concern. Coaching can also provide an opportunity to invest in your personal development, wellbeing, and future direction.

What can coaching help me with?

Coaching can help you explore a range of personal and professional topics, depending on what matters to you.

This might include building confidence, managing change, developing self-awareness, exploring work-life balance, recognising your strengths, considering career or life choices, or identifying what you would like to achieve.

Everyone's circumstances are different, and coaching is shaped around your individual needs, priorities and aspirations.

Do I need to know what I want to achieve before starting?

Not at all!

You might arrive with a particular question or challenge, or you may simply feel that you need some time to think.

Coaching can help you explore what's important to you, what is working well, and what you might like to change. There is no expectation that you arrive with a clear goal or a plan.

We will work together to find a focus that feels meaningful to you.

What is the difference between coaching and a wellbeing walk?

Our wellbeing walks offer an opportunity to spend time outdoors, enjoy nature, connect with others and make space for your wellbeing. You don't need to have a particular goal or concern to take part.

Coaching offers a more focused opportunity to explore something that matters to you, whether that's a decision, a challenge, a personal aspiration or a change you would like to make.

Both provide opportunities to pause and reflect, but coaching offers a more structured, personalised process to help you explore your own way forward.

For more information, please see our Wellbeing Walks Frequently Asked Questions.

What is the difference between coaching and a wellbeing retreat?

Our wellbeing retreats offer time away from everyday routines to enjoy the outdoors, connect with nature, meet new people and reflect on what matters to you. They provide a broader wellbeing experience over two days and one night.

Coaching offers a more personalised opportunity to explore your own circumstances, priorities and aspirations through dedicated coaching conversations.

Both share our commitment to wellbeing, reflection and personal growth, but they offer different ways to make time for yourself.

For more information, please see our Wellbeing Retreats Frequently Asked Questions.

Is coaching the same as counselling or therapy?

No. Conscience Coaching and Consulting offers non-clinical coaching informed by a person-centred, solution-focused approach.

Coaching provides an opportunity to reflect, recognise your strengths, explore possibilities, and consider practical steps towards your goals. It is not a substitute for counselling, psychotherapy, or clinical mental health treatment. It can, however, be complementary to such interventions.

If you need clinical or specialist support, we encourage you to seek help from an appropriately qualified professional.

What happens during an introductory chemistry session?

The introductory chemistry session is an opportunity for us to meet, talk about what has brought you to coaching, and explore what you hope to gain from the experience.

It also gives us both the chance to consider whether we feel comfortable working together.

A positive coaching relationship is built on trust, mutual respect, and a shared understanding of what you would like to explore.

There is no pressure to commit. The aim is to help you decide whether coaching with Conscience feels right for you.

How many coaching sessions will I need?

Coaching is generally offered as a series of up to six one-hour sessions, with the arrangements agreed together.

The number of sessions and the focus of our work will depend on your circumstances, needs, and goals. We will take time to reflect on what you are learning, what feels helpful, and what you would like to explore next.

The aim is to support you in finding your own way forward, at a pace that works for you.

Do you offer coaching online or indoors?

Absolutely! Through our own research and growing evidence of outdoors creating a different coaching space, it is not for everyone.

Coaching provides an opportunity to reflect, recognise your strengths, explore possibilities, and consider practical steps towards your goals. Creating the right conditions – where you feel safe – is essential, and this can be in-person indoors, outdoors or virtually.

Please contact Angela on 07763 242570 or at angela@consciencecoaching.co.uk to explore the different options.

Where do outdoor coaching sessions take place?

Nature-based coaching offers an opportunity to take your coaching conversations outdoors, where appropriate.

Sessions may take place in an agreed outdoor setting, such as a park, woodland, coastal path or countryside location. Being outdoors can offer a change of scenery and a different perspective, creating space to pause and reflect.

We will discuss suitable locations and arrangements beforehand, taking account of your needs, preferences, and accessibility requirements.

Do I need to be fit or an experienced walker?

Not at all! You don't need to be an experienced walker to benefit from nature-based coaching.

The focus is on the coaching conversation, not on how far or how fast you walk. We can discuss the location, terrain, pace and any adjustments that may help you feel comfortable.

If you have accessibility requirements, mobility considerations or concerns about a particular location, please get in touch before your session so we can explore what may be possible.

Will I be expected to talk about personal experiences of feelings?

No. Coaching offers space for reflection, but there is no pressure to share anything you would prefer to keep private.

You can explore your thoughts and experiences at your own pace. Our role is to listen, ask questions and support you in considering what matters to you, without judgement or telling you what you should do.

You remain in control of what you choose to discuss.

Will what I share during coaching remain confidential?

Trust and confidentiality are important foundations of the coaching relationship. We will discuss confidentiality and its boundaries before coaching begins.

As with other professional support arrangements, there may be circumstances in which information needs to be shared because of serious safety concerns or legal obligations.

These boundaries will be explained as part of our initial agreement.

For outdoor sessions, we will also consider practical privacy and choose a suitable setting wherever possible.

What if I feel nervous about starting coaching?

That's completely understandable! Starting something new or taking time to focus on yourself can sometimes feel daunting.

You don't need to arrive feeling confident or have everything figured out. The introductory chemistry session gives us an opportunity to talk through your questions, explore what you are looking for, and decide whether coaching feels right for you.

There is no pressure to commit. We can start with a conversation and take things from there.

What happens between coaching sessions?

Coaching can encourage you to reflect on your conversations and consider how your learning might relate to everyday life.

You might notice what is working well, try a different approach, reflect on a question or take a small step towards something that matters to you.

The focus is on helping you recognise your own insights and identify practical steps that feel appropriate for your circumstances. Any arrangements for support between sessions will be discussed and agreed as part of your coaching package.

What happens if I find a coaching conversation difficult or emotional?

Reflecting on your experiences, decisions or aspirations can sometimes bring up difficult feelings. There is no expectation that you share more than you feel comfortable with.

We can pause, change direction, or take time to consider what feels helpful. The aim is to provide a respectful, supportive space where you can explore your thoughts at your own pace.

If your needs fall outside the scope of coaching, we can discuss the importance of accessing appropriate specialist support.

How much does coaching cost?

Coaching fees and arrangements will depend on the service and package agreed.

Accessibility matters to us, and we are committed to ensuring our services are as accessible as possible within our operational boundaries.

Please get in touch to discuss the available options, costs, and any funding opportunities that may be available. We can talk through your circumstances and help you understand the arrangements before you decide whether to proceed.

Can coaching be arranged for organisations, teams, or groups?

Yes! We also offer wellbeing and development support for organisations, teams and groups.

If you are interested in exploring coaching or related wellbeing activities for your organisation, please get in touch to discuss your needs, the outcomes you hope to achieve, and the options that may be suitable.

We would be happy to have a conversation and help you explore the next steps.

Still have a question?

We want you to feel comfortable and informed before starting coaching. If you have any questions or would like to talk through your circumstances, please get in touch with Angela.

You don't need to have everything figured out. Sometimes, giving yourself permission to pause and think is a positive first step.
